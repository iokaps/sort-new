# Sorting Mania - Host Guide 🎮

## What is Sorting Mania?

Sorting Mania is a fast-paced, wildly competitive sorting game where players become speed demons, frantically swiping cards left and right to categorize items before time runs out. Think of it as a "swipe battle royale" for your brain. It's chaotic, it's fun, and everyone leaves wanting to play again.

### Who Is This For?

This guide is for **event facilitators, classroom instructors, and team leaders** who want to run an interactive, competitive game that actually gets people engaged and having fun. You don't need technical expertise—just a computer, a link to share, and the ability to handle the friendly chaos that ensues.

### When & Where Sorting Mania Works Best 🎯

- **Classroom Settings**: The perfect icebreaker or brain break. Sorting historical figures? Animals? Elements? This game makes learning competitive and fun.
- **Team Building Events**: Forget awkward trust falls. Get your team sorted (literally!).
- **Game Nights**: Remote hangouts, in-person parties, or anywhere you want friendly competition and lots of laughs.
- **Training Sessions**: Make boring training actually memorable. People will talk about the sorting competition more than the actual content. You're welcome.
- **Any Group Activity**: Works amazingly with 2–50+ participants. The sweet spot? 15–30 people.

## Getting Started as a Host 🚀

### Prerequisites

You'll need:
- ✅ A computer with internet access
- ✅ A projector, TV, or shared screen (optional but *highly* recommended—watching the live leaderboard updates is where the real drama happens)
- ✅ Players with their own devices (phones, tablets, or computers). The more, the merrier!

### How to Access the Game

1. **Open the Game Link**  
   Your facilitator/administrator will provide you with a link to Sorting Mania on the Kokimoki platform. Click that link and you're in.

2. **Welcome to the Command Center**  
   You're automatically placed in "Host Mode," which means you're now in complete control of the chaos. Power trip? Totally justified.

3. **Spread the Word**  
   You'll see a **Player Join Link**. Share it with your participants via email, text, QR code, or by screaming it from the rooftops (projecting it on screen is less dramatic but equally effective).

## Step-by-Step: Running the Game ⚡

### Phase 1: Setup (2–5 minutes)

When you first enter, you're in the **Setup Phase**. This is where the magic starts. Here's what to do:

#### 🤖 Option A: Let AI Do the Heavy Lifting (Easiest)

1. **Enter a Theme**  
   Type something your group will get excited about (e.g., "Movie genres," "Dinosaur types," "Types of pizza," "Superheroes")

2. **Click "Generate with AI"**  
   The AI works its magic and creates:
   - Two sorting categories
   - 20–30 items auto-categorized for you

3. **Sanity Check**  
   Glance at it. Does it make sense? Are the categories fun? If yes, proceed. If not, hit "generate" again—the AI's got plenty of ideas.

4. **Set the Speed**  
   How long should your players have to sort? (30–120 seconds)
   - **First time?** 60 seconds. Good balance of fun and challenge.
   - **Want chaos?** 30 seconds. Things get spicy.
   - **Want to ease them in?** 90 seconds. Merciful.

5. **Click "Start Game"** 🎬

#### 👨‍💼 Option B: Build It Yourself (For Creative Control)

If you want total control or have a super specific theme in mind:

1. **Enter a Theme**  
   What's the context? (e.g., "Music artists," "Basketball vs. Football players," "Pizza toppings I hate")

2. **Enter Two Categories**  
   The left vs. right split. Be creative! (e.g., "Coffee" vs. "Tea," "Movies" vs. "TV Shows")

3. **Add Your Items**  
   List what players will sort, and pick the correct category for each one. Shoot for 20–30 items. The more, the longer the round.

4. **Pick Your Duration**  
   How much time should they have? (See "Set the Speed" above for guidance)

5. **Click "Start Game"** 🎬

### Phase 2: Lobby (1–2 minutes) 🏟️

The game is holding, waiting for your players to show up.

**Your Screen:**
- A live roster of everyone who's joined with their names
- A big "Start Game" button just begging to be clicked

**Their Screen:**
- Name entry field
- A lobby showing who else has joined (the hype builds!)
- Waiting for you to begin

**Your Move:**
- Wait for your crew to arrive (names pop up in real-time—it's like a guest list!)
- Once everyone's there, you're ready to go
- **Click "Start Game"** and let the madness begin

---

### Phase 3: Game Play (30–120 seconds) 🔥

**It's live. It's intense. It's happening NOW.**

**Your Screen (The Hall of Fame... and Shame):**
- Real-time leaderboard with live scores updating
- A timer counting down to chaos
- Each player's sort count

**Their Screen (The Battle Arena):**
- A card floating in the center with an item
- Two category names on the left and right (the two sides of destiny)
- Timer ticking away
- Progress bar showing how many they've sorted

**What's Happening in Real-Time:**
- Players swipe LEFT → matches the left category
- Players swipe RIGHT → matches the right category
- **Correct sort?** +1 point 🎉
- **Wrong swipe?** 0 points 😅
- Each player gets unique random item order (no two experiences are the same)
- Pure speed and accuracy competition

**Your Job:**
- Watch the leaderboard like a hawk
- Narrate the action out loud if you want (highly recommended for entertainment)
- Let the timer do its thing
- When time hits zero, the game auto-transitions to results

---

### Phase 4: Results (It's Glory Time!) 🏆

The timer hits zero and everyone gets taken to the **Results Screen**.

**Your Screen (The Moment of Truth):**
- **The Podium** — top 3 players in glorious detail
- **Full Leaderboard** — everyone's scores ranked
- Stats and bragging rights

**Their Screen:**
- How they placed
- Exactly how many they got right (or how badly they bombed)
- The full rankings
- The realization they should've played faster

**What's Next:**
You've got options:
- **🔄 Run Another Round**: New theme, same crew—rematches are where legends are born
- **🎨 Switch It Up**: Try a completely different theme or topic
- **🎬 Wrap It Up**: End the session and let everyone recover

Pick your move and click the button.

## Pro Tips for Maximum Fun 💡

### Before the Game

- **Preview your content** — Nothing kills vibes like a weird generated category. Give it a quick look.
- **Clear, simple categories** — Confusion = frustration. Make the categories immediately obvious (not "Earthling" and "Extraterrestrial").
- **Give the rules quick overview**: "Swipe left for left, swipe right for right. GO FAST. Have fun." That's it.
- **Pick difficulty wisely**: Easy topic + short time = chaos. Harder topic + longer time = let them think. Balance it.

### During the Game

- **Be a hype person** — Announce close races, call out the leader, build the drama. You're a sports commentator now.
- **Project that leaderboard** — If possible, display it on a big screen. The real-time updates and jaw-dropping moments are PEAK entertainment.
- **Run multiple rounds** — One round is fun. Two or three rounds? People are hooked. Competitive rematches = magic.

### Hosting Like a Pro

- **Start easy** — Give them one gentle warm-up round so they understand the mechanics
- **Escalate** — Make themes trickier or time tighter in later rounds. Build tension.
- **Keep it light** — This is a game, not a standardized test. Wrong answers are hilarious.
- **Crown your champions** — Make a big deal out of the winners. They've earned it.

## Troubleshooting (AKA, "Why Isn't It Working?") 🔧

| Issue | What To Do |
|-------|----------|
| **"I have no link!"** | Copy the player link from your host screen and re-share it. Shout it, email it, project it. Whatever works. |
| **Player arrives nameless** | They'll get nagged with a prompt to enter their name. Can't proceed without it. |
| **Content is... weird** | No problem. Stop, regenerate, start fresh. Any in-progress scores reset (sorry, not sorry). |
| **Everyone's timer looks different** | Nope—they're all synced to the server. What you see is what's real everywhere. |
| **Someone got booted mid-game** | They can log back in with the same link and rejoin the leaderboard. No permanent damage. |

## FAQ (The Burning Questions) 🔥

**Q: Can I run multiple games at once?**  
A: One game per link. Want multiple simultaneous games? You'd need separate links. Logistics, sorry.

**Q: How many players can I throw at this?**  
A: 2 people? Sure. 100 people? Also works! Sweet spot for maximum vibes? 15–30. Beyond that, it *still* works but the energy diffuses.

**Q: Someone's super late—can they still join?**  
A: Yep! They jump in during lobby or even mid-game. Score starts at 0, but they're on the leaderboard and can catch up. No permanent marking on their record.

**Q: Can we use pictures instead of text?**  
A: Not right now—it's text-based. Want images? Mention it to your administrator. Future feature perhaps?

**Q: What if a player has no idea what a category means?**  
A: That's the beauty! It's about speed AND accuracy, but guessing is 100% allowed. Wrong answers are half the fun.

**Q: Do I need wifi, or can we run this offline?**  
A: WiFi required. Sorting Mania lives on the Kokimoki platform in the cloud. No internet = no game. Plan accordingly.
